SSP

Single-cell spatial pharmacobiology identifies conserved stromal barriers to therapeutic antibody delivery in human solid tumors

Open paper Code

SSP 方法中文解读

一句话概括

SSP（single-cell spatial pharmacobiology，单细胞空间药理生物学）把“临床给药后的抗体在哪里、碰到了哪些细胞、是否接触到靶点、又被哪些肿瘤微环境结构限制”这几个问题放在同一张组织切片里测量。本文用 panitumumab-IRDye800（pan800）作为带荧光的 EGFR 靶向抗体，在 HNSCC 和 PDAC 人源肿瘤样本中展示这个框架。

它想解决什么问题

传统药理学常见的测量尺度太粗。血浆 PK 可以告诉我们血液里有多少药，PET/SPECT 可以告诉我们器官层面哪里有信号，但这些方法很难回答：

  • 抗体是否真正进入肿瘤内部；
  • EGFR 阳性的肿瘤细胞是否真的接触到抗体；
  • 高 ECM、CAF、血管或缺氧区域是否改变了抗体进入肿瘤的机会；
  • 同一个患者内部不同区域的药物可达性是否差异很大。

SSP 的核心思想是把药物信号和空间组学信号配准到同一套单细胞坐标上，从而把“药物递送”变成一个可以按细胞类型、肿瘤边界、ECM 邻域和 CAF 邻域分层分析的空间问题。

方法流程

第一步是临床扰动。患者在手术前接受系统性注射的 pan800。手术切下来的肿瘤组织中，pan800 的近红外信号代表抗体在组织中的实际分布。

第二步是组织成像。研究者先获取 pan800 和核染色图像，再对同一组织进行 CODEX 多重空间蛋白组学成像。CODEX 抗体面板覆盖肿瘤细胞、免疫细胞、CAF、血管、ECM、EGFR 和细胞状态标志物。

第三步是图像预处理和配准。CODEX 原始图像由 RAPID 处理，包括去卷积、最佳焦平面选择、拼接、漂移校正、背景扣除和 hyperstack 生成。然后 paper 描述了 pan800 图像和 CODEX 图像之间的跨平台配准：先用核图像做归一化和相位相关得到初始变换，再做 similarity transform 和 diffeomorphic demons refinement，最后把同样的变换应用到 pan800 药物图像上。这个步骤非常关键，因为如果配准不准，后面把 pan800 分配给单个细胞就会出错。

第四步是单细胞表构建。研究者用 Mesmer 做 whole-cell 和 nuclear segmentation，然后按细胞大小或细胞核大小归一化蛋白强度和 pan800 强度。EGFR 和 pan800 的阈值不是一个抽象模型学出来的，而是结合 EGFR 阴性、pan800 阴性参考细胞类型设定，并回到图像上检查。

第五步是空间结构分析。SSP 同时定义两类邻域：

  • ECM neighborhood：以每个 anchor cell 周围 25 um 为窗口，计算 Collagen I、Collagen IV、Fibronectin、Tenascin C、Periostin 五种 ECM 标志物的面积分数，再用 MiniBatchKMeans 聚类。HNSCC 中 k=4，PDAC 中 k=5。
  • Cellular neighborhood（CN）：以中心细胞和最近邻细胞组成窗口，统计窗口内不同细胞类型的频率，再用 MiniBatchKMeans 得到局部细胞组成模式。HNSCC 中 window size=9、k=10；PDAC 中 window size=10、k=14。

第六步是统计关联。paper 主要在 TMA core / tissue region 层面做 Pearson correlation、BH 校正、Mann-Whitney U test，并做 participant-aware robustness analysis。这个设计很重要，因为多个区域可能来自同一位患者，不能简单把所有 core 当成完全独立样本解释。

关键结果怎么理解

SSP 首先证明 pan800 不是均匀进入肿瘤的。不同癌种、患者、组织区域、细胞类型之间都有明显差异。在 HNSCC 中，pan800 较高的区域与 pEGFR 较低相关，说明 pan800 不只是“到没到”的信号，也可以作为局部 EGFR target engagement 的线索。不过 paper 也明确提醒：pan800 是 subtherapeutic imaging dose，因此 pEGFR 更适合解释为局部相对药效读数，而不是完整治疗剂量下的通路抑制证明。

最核心的发现是：periostin-rich ECM 和 FAP-positive CAF neighborhood 与较低抗体递送相关。

在 HNSCC 中，periostin、collagen、fibronectin 等 ECM 不是随机分布，而是形成局部 ECM neighborhood。某些 periostin-rich ECM neighborhood 出现在血管周围或肿瘤边界附近，并与更低的 pan800 信号相关。FAP-positive 或 FAP-positive CD73-positive CAF neighborhood 又与这些 ECM-rich 结构相连，提示 CAF-ECM 空间组合可能构成抗体进入肿瘤的局部屏障。

在 PDAC 中，肿瘤本身更纤维化，ECM 和 CAF 更丰富。paper 发现 periostin/collagen-rich pECM2、tumor-proximal FAP-positive CAF interface 与 pan800 较低方向一致。这里要谨慎：PDAC 某些分析限定在 50 mg dose 的小队列，因此作者把它解释为和 HNSCC 方向一致的支持证据，而不是独立充分证明。

Xenium 空间转录组验证进一步支持这个模型：在肿瘤邻近 FAP-positive CAF 中，POSTN 表达与 tumor pan800 呈负相关，HNSCC 和 PDAC 的方向一致。

公开代码能复现到什么程度

不能把公开代码解读为完整 SSP 复现包。Nature Code availability 给出的是多个组件仓库，而不是一个端到端 SSP pipeline。

比较强的公开代码对应关系包括：

  • RAPID：能对应 CODEX 图像预处理组件；
  • ECM_neighborhoods：HNSCC ECM neighborhood 的五个 ECM feature 和 MiniBatchKMeans k=4 与 paper 方法高度一致；
  • NeighborhoodCoordination / TissueSchematics：提供细胞邻域和空间示意图相关的通用工具；
  • deepcell-tf：提供 DeepCell / segmentation 生态的通用库支持。

但是，很多 SSP 专属步骤没有在获取到的公开代码中找到，包括 pan800-CODEX 跨平台配准与 TRE/DSC 评估、pan800/EGFR gate、spillover compensation、tumor nest segmentation、Xenium POSTN/FAP 验证脚本和最终统计脚本。因此，这篇 paper 的可复现性更像“核心组件可核查，完整 workflow 不可直接重跑”。

方法的价值和边界

SSP 的价值在于把抗体药理学从“一个患者或一个肿瘤的平均值”推进到“组织里哪些细胞和空间结构让药物到达或到不了”。它特别适合用来发现空间药物递送屏障、解释局部耐药机制、辅助早期临床试验中的剂量和 biomarker 设计。

它的边界也很清楚：本文主要是空间关联研究，不是 ECM 或 CAF 扰动实验。periostin-rich ECM 和 FAP-positive CAF 与低 pan800 相关，但不能单独由这些图证明它们在每个肿瘤里都是因果屏障。要把这个模型变成干预策略，还需要后续 perturbation 或治疗组合实验。

SSP: Single-Cell Spatial Pharmacobiology

Paper

Title: Single-cell spatial pharmacobiology identifies conserved stromal barriers to therapeutic antibody delivery in human solid tumors

Venue / year: Nature Biotechnology, 2026

DOI: 10.1038/s41587-026-03152-x

Local source: paper source/paper/hybrid_auto/paper.md

Motivation

Therapeutic antibodies can fail in solid tumors even when the molecular target is present. Standard pharmacology measurements leave a spatial gap: plasma assays and PET/SPECT imaging can measure systemic or organ-level exposure, but they cannot show whether an antibody reaches target-positive tumor cells, whether target signaling changes locally, or which tumor microenvironment structures block delivery.

SSP addresses this by measuring antibody delivery, target engagement and local tissue architecture in intact human tumor sections at single-cell resolution. The study uses panitumumab-IRDye800 (pan800), an EGFR-targeting antibody conjugate, in surgical-window phase 1 clinical-trial tissues from HNSCC and PDAC.

Method Overview

SSP is an experimental and computational framework, not a single model. Its main workflow is:

  1. Infuse a fluorescently labeled therapeutic antibody before tumor resection.
  2. Image pan800 signal in tumor tissue with high-resolution near-infrared microscopy.
  3. Profile the same tissue by CODEX spatial proteomics with markers for tumor cells, CAFs, immune cells, vasculature, ECM, EGFR and cell states.
  4. Register pan800 images to CODEX images so drug signal can be assigned to segmented cells.
  5. Segment cells with Mesmer, quantify marker and pan800 intensity per cell, and identify cell types.
  6. Build ECM neighborhoods from five ECM marker area fractions in 25 um pericellular windows.
  7. Build cellular neighborhoods from nearest-neighbor cell-type frequency windows.
  8. Relate pan800 delivery and target engagement to tumor topology, ECM neighborhoods, CAF neighborhoods and region-level statistics.

The key computational objects are single-cell drug/protein tables, tumor-nest spatial context, ECM neighborhood labels, cellular neighborhood labels, CN-CN interface summaries and region-level correlation tests. The paper also defines registration accuracy checks using TRE and Dice-style overlap metrics in the supplement.

Key Results

SSP shows that antibody delivery is highly heterogeneous across tumor types, participants, tissue regions and cell types. The clinical material comes from phase 1 pan800 surgical-window trials: pan800 was administered to 50 participants with HNSCC, PDAC or NSCLC, and the main bulk tumor comparison reports HNSCC n=25, NSCLC n=4 and PDAC n=14. In the spatial proteomics analysis, the main HNSCC CODEX cohort includes 18 participants, and the PDAC CODEX cohort includes 12 participants.

The paper validates several measurement baselines and quality checks before interpreting spatial biology. Mesmer segmentation in fibroblast-rich regions is benchmarked against manual annotations, with F1 scores around 0.82 in both HNSCC and PDAC. Pan800-CODEX registration reports mean TRE 0.97 +/- 0.41 um and refined nuclear DSC 0.92 +/- 0.05 across 111 regions. These checks support the central premise that drug signal can be assigned to single cells and spatial neighborhoods.

In HNSCC, pan800 signal is inversely associated with pEGFR at the region level (Pearson r=-0.47, P=0.0082, n=30 regions), supporting pan800 as a local target-engagement readout rather than only a delivery marker.

The major biological finding is that stromal structure, especially periostin-rich ECM and FAP-positive CAF neighborhoods, is associated with reduced antibody delivery. In HNSCC, periostin/collagen/fibronectin-rich ECM neighborhoods around vessels and tumor nests correlate with lower pan800. FAP-positive and FAP-positive CD73-positive CAF neighborhoods align with ECM-rich spatial niches and reduced tumor access.

In PDAC, which has denser fibrotic stroma, the same direction appears: periostin-rich / collagen-rich ECM neighborhoods and tumor-proximal FAP-positive CAF interfaces are associated with lower pan800. In the 50 mg PDAC dosing cohort, periostin area fraction is negatively associated with tumor pan800 (r=-0.54, P=0.02), and pECM2 frequency around tumor regions is also negatively associated with tumor pan800 (r=-0.62, BH-adjusted P=0.03). The PDAC analysis is more limited in participant number and is presented as directional concordance with the larger HNSCC cohort, not as an equally powered replication.

Orthogonal Xenium spatial transcriptomics supports the model by showing inverse associations between tumor pan800 and POSTN expression in tumor-proximal FAP-positive CAFs, with consistent direction in both HNSCC (r=-0.92, BH-adjusted P=0.0002) and PDAC (r=-0.80, BH-adjusted P=0.001).

Code Availability and Reproducibility

Reproducibility rating: 3 / 5.

The paper provides multiple public component repositories but no monolithic SSP implementation. The strongest public-code match is RAPID for CODEX preprocessing. The ECM neighborhood notebook strongly matches the HNSCC MiniBatchKMeans ECM-neighborhood step from precomputed ECM features. NeighborhoodCoordination and TissueSchematics provide generic cellular-neighborhood and visualization tooling. DeepCell provides a broad segmentation-library match for Mesmer-style segmentation.

Major SSP-specific analysis code was not found in the acquired public snapshots: pan800-CODEX registration and TRE/DSC validation scripts, pan800/EGFR quantification gates, inverse-adjacency spillover compensation, tumor-nest segmentation, Xenium POSTN/FAP validation scripts and final statistics. A reader can inspect important computational building blocks, but cannot replay the full clinical spatial pharmacobiology workflow from public code alone.

See doc_code.md for the detailed component-by-component match assessment.

Strengths

  • Directly measures infused antibody distribution in human tumor tissue rather than relying only on model systems or systemic imaging.
  • Integrates drug imaging, target marker state, multiplexed spatial proteomics and spatial transcriptomics validation.
  • Uses single-cell and region-level spatial analyses to connect drug access with ECM and CAF architecture.
  • Distinguishes delivery, target expression and local target engagement instead of collapsing them into one bulk readout.
  • Explicitly checks participant-aware robustness for major region-level associations.

Limitations and Caveats

  • The study is observational within clinical-window tissue; periostin-rich ECM and FAP-positive CAFs are associated with reduced delivery, but the paper does not perturb these structures to prove causality.
  • PDAC support is directionally consistent but smaller and dose-restricted for several analyses.
  • Several computational steps are custom and not publicly reproducible from the released code.
  • Public component repositories can be mistaken for a complete SSP pipeline; they should be interpreted as partial evidence only.
  • Thresholding, segmentation, tumor-nest masks and registration accuracy are central to downstream conclusions, so missing implementation details matter for independent reproduction.

Practical Takeaway

SSP is best understood as a spatial pharmacology assay and analysis framework for clinical tumor tissues. Its main contribution is making antibody delivery and target engagement local: the method asks not only whether a drug reaches a tumor, but which single cells and stromal neighborhoods it reaches, and which conserved ECM/CAF niches appear to restrict access.
