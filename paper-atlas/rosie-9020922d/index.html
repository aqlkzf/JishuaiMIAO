ROSIE: AI generation of multiplex immunofluorescence staining from histopathology images

Open paper Code

ROSIE 方法解释：从常规 H&E 推断多重免疫荧光

一句话抓住方法

ROSIE 把一张 H&E 图像切成带有局部组织上下文的 $128\times128$ 像素块，用 ImageNet 预训练的 ConvNeXt-Small 一次回归 50 个蛋白标志物在中心 $8\times8$ 区域的平均表达；推断时按 8 像素步长铺满组织并拼回多通道图像，再可借助细胞分割把像素级预测汇总为单细胞表达。

1. 它解决的不是“重新染色”，而是条件估计

H&E 便宜、普及，能显示细胞核、细胞质和组织形态，却不直接给出 CD45、CD8、PanCK 等特定蛋白。CODEX/mIF 能同时测几十种蛋白，但成本和实验门槛更高。ROSIE 学习的是配准后的同一组织切片中

\[f_\theta:\text{H\&E morphology}\longrightarrow\text{expected multiplex protein expression}.\]

因此输出是模型从形态推断的表达，不是实际测量，也不能把“看起来相似”理解为发现了 H&E 中原本不存在的确定分子事实。

2. 配对数据怎样变成监督信号

论文使用同片共染并配准的 H&E–CODEX 样本。训练集覆盖 18 项研究、1342 个样本和超过 1600 万个细胞。对每个空间位置：

  1. 在 CODEX 图上取中心 $8\times8$ 区域，对各标志物求平均，得到目标向量 $\mathbf y\in\mathbb R^{50}$；
  2. 在同一中心截取更大的 $128\times128$ H&E 图块，让模型同时看到中心及其邻域；
  3. 将 H&E 图块缩放到 $224\times224$，训练时加入翻转、旋转、颜色扰动和 ImageNet 标准化；
  4. ConvNeXt-Small 的分类头被替换为 50 维线性回归头。

这里“128 输入、8 输出”很关键：模型不是为每个输入像素生成一个蛋白像素，而是用较大形态上下文估计中心小区域的平均表达。论文指出 40× 扫描下该上下文通常约含 30 个细胞，约覆盖三跳细胞邻域。

3. 为什么需要 masked MSE

不同研究测量的抗体面板不完全相同。令 $m_j=1$ 表示第 $j$ 个标志物在当前样本中真实测得，否则为 0，则训练损失可写为

\[\mathcal L(\theta)= \frac{\sum_j m_j\left(\hat y_j-y_j\right)^2} {\sum_j m_j}.\]

例如某图块只测了三个标志物，真实值为 $(0.2,0.7,0.4)$，预测为 $(0.3,0.5,0.9)$，但第三项缺测，掩码为 $(1,1,0)$，损失就是 $[(0.3-0.2)^2+(0.5-0.7)^2]/2=0.025$；第三项不会被当成零值监督。代码在 train.py:106-123 先用布尔掩码选出有效元素，再计算均方误差。

4. 从图块预测到组织图和单细胞表

标准推断以 8 像素为步长滑动。每个位置产生一个 50 维向量，并填入对应的 $8\times8$ 区域；所有位置拼接后得到 50 通道的连续预测图。论文也展示 1 像素步长的高分辨率视觉结果，但计算量约为标准设置的 64 倍，正文定量分析使用 8 像素设置。

若有细胞分割，后处理对每个细胞掩膜内的预测像素求通道均值：

\[\hat e_{c,j}=\frac{1}{|S_c|}\sum_{p\in S_c}\hat I_j(p),\]

其中 $S_c$ 是细胞 $c$ 的分割区域。这样得到的“细胞 × 标志物”矩阵才进入 kNN 细胞分型、组织结构发现和邻域表型分析。

5. 怎样读论文的验证链

  • 表达层：四个评估数据集、50 个标志物的总体 Pearson $R=0.285$、Spearman $R=0.352$、样本级 C-index $=0.706$。这些是中等相关而非逐像素完美重建，且标志物间差异很大。
  • 细胞层：用真实 CODEX 建立的 kNN 标签规则接收 ROSIE 表达，七类细胞的 F1 均优于批量比例随机分配和形态特征基线；B/T 细胞区分尤其重要。
  • 组织层：SCGP 分别在真实与预测表达上划分空间结构，ROSIE 的平均 ARI 为 0.475、F1 为 0.624，高于形态基线的 0.105 和 0.229。
  • 邻域层：预测的淋巴细胞邻近上皮细胞比例与真值相关 $R=0.598$，肿瘤浸润淋巴细胞数量相关 $R=0.805$。

这条链的意义是：作者没有只比较彩色图是否“像”，而是继续测试预测表达是否保留细胞身份、组织结构和免疫邻域信号。

6. 没有真值时怎样做质量控制

论文提出两个启发式指标：

  • H&E 分布偏移：把测试图与训练 H&E 的 256-bin 强度直方图计算 W1（Wasserstein）距离，再对训练图平均；距离大意味着输入更偏离训练分布。
  • 预测通道动态范围：$P_{99}-P_1$；范围过窄常对应低质量生成。

它们是风险筛查器，不是准确率证书。按中位数过滤后总体 Pearson $R$ 从 0.285 分别升到 0.312（W1）和 0.336（动态范围），但真实部署仍需外部校准。

8. 使用边界

原始训练数据因许可和隐私限制未公开，模型权重需向作者申请。当前本地代码是脚本快照：路径、数据集划分和部分依赖需要手工配置；train.py:273-343 的 evaluate() 返回 None，而训练循环在 train.py:458-481 将其解包为两个指标，因此原样运行会在验证阶段失败。它足以核对模型、损失、拼图与细胞汇总机制，但不是论文结果的开箱即跑复现包。

跨站点、染色流程或成像平台的批次差异仍是主要风险；论文在 Orion-CRC 上也观察到部分标志物（如 ECad）泛化较差。最稳妥的定位是：ROSIE 提供可筛查、可排序、可辅助下游分析的 in silico mIF 候选信号，而不是替代真实免疫染色的临床定论。

ROSIE: RObust in Silico Immunofluorescence from H&E Images

Paper Overview

Title: ROSIE: AI generation of multiplex immunofluorescence staining from histopathology images Published: Nature Communications, August 2025 Authors: Eric Wu, Matthew Bieniosek, Zhengin Wu, Nitya Thakkar, et al. (Enable Medicine, Stanford University) Code: https://gitlab.com/enable-medicine-public/rosie Contact: eric@enablemedicine.com, alex@enablemedicine.com

Core Problem & Motivation

H&E (Hematoxylin & Eosin) staining is ubiquitous, inexpensive, and clinically standard but cannot directly reveal molecular markers. Multiplex immunofluorescence (mIF) like CODEX can detect dozens of proteins simultaneously but is costly, time-intensive, and not widely available in clinical settings.

“Pathologists can identify diverse cell types from H&E staining alone; however, computational approaches to annotating H&E images only distinguish a few broad cell categories, such as endothelial, epithelial, stromal, and immune cells.” (Page 1)

Gap addressed: Previous methods predicted only 1-10 biomarkers from small datasets with limited biological validation.

Key Innovations

  1. Scale: Largest co-stained H&E-CODEX dataset (1342 training samples, 16M+ cells, 18 studies)
  2. Multi-marker: Simultaneously predicts 50 protein biomarkers (vs. 1-10 in prior work)
  3. Diversity: Spans 10 body areas and 16 disease types
  4. Biological validation: Not just prediction accuracy but cell phenotyping and tissue structure discovery
  5. Simple architecture: ConvNeXt-Small CNN (50M params) outperforms larger Vision Transformers

Method Summary

Model Architecture

Core: ConvNeXt-Small CNN pre-trained on ImageNet-1K

# From train.py:139-141
model = models.convnext_small(weights='IMAGENET1K_V1')
model.classifier[2] = nn.Linear(model.classifier[2].in_features, num_outputs)  # 50 outputs

Prediction Strategy

  • Input: 128 x 128 pixel H&E patch
  • Output: Average expression of 50 biomarkers for center 8 x 8 pixel region
  • Reconstruction: Sliding window (8px stride) stitches predictions into full image

“Given an input 128 x 128 pixel patch, it produces a prediction for the average expressions of the biomarker panel across the center 8 x 8 pixels.” (Page 2)

Loss Function: Masked MSE

Different studies use different biomarker panels, so a masked loss handles missing biomarkers:

\[L = \frac{1}{|M|}\sum_{i \in M}(y_i - \hat{y}_i)^2\]

where $M$ is the set of valid (non-missing) biomarkers for each sample.

# From train.py:106-123
def masked_mse_loss(pred, target, mask):
    mask = mask.bool()
    masked_pred = torch.masked_select(pred, mask)
    masked_target = torch.masked_select(target, mask)
    return F.mse_loss(masked_pred, masked_target, reduction='mean')

Quality Control Metrics

Two heuristics for filtering low-quality predictions:

  1. Dynamic Range: $DR = P_{99} - P_{1}$ (difference between 99th and 1st percentile)
    • Low dynamic range indicates poor prediction quality
  2. Wasserstein Distance (W1): Average distance between test and training image histograms
    • High W1 indicates out-of-distribution sample

Using median cutoffs improves average Pearson R from 0.285 to:

  • 0.312 (W1 filtering)
  • 0.336 (dynamic range filtering)

Key Findings

1. CNN Outperforms Transformer

“ViT-L/16 Transformer model with over 300 M parameters pre-trained on histopathology images still did not perform as well as ConvNext, a 50 M parameter CNN.” (Page 6)

Reasons:

  • Convolutional inductive bias suits local feature extraction in pathology
  • Larger models overfit earlier during training
  • Patch-based prediction benefits from local context

2. Training on Full 50-marker Panel Helps

Even when evaluating on fewer markers, training on all 50 improves performance due to information sharing between related biomarkers in the shared embedding space.

3. Simple MSE Loss Beats GANs

Pix2pix GAN significantly underperforms ROSIE:

  • GANs have training instability with large biomarker panels
  • Prior GAN methods focused on visual metrics (SSIM) rather than biological accuracy
  • Single MSE objective is more stable

4. Immunological Validity

The model correctly distinguishes between immunologically “hot” and “cold” tumors:

  • Stanford-PGC (pancreatic cancer, “cold”): 20.0% predicted T cells (21.1% ground truth)
  • Ochsner-CRC (colorectal cancer, “hot”): 40.1% predicted T cells (30.6% ground truth)

Limitations

  1. Context window: 128x128 px (~30 cells) may miss longer-range tissue context
  2. Batch effects: Sensitive to H&E staining variations across sites
  3. Biomarker performance varies: Some immune markers harder to predict
  4. Platform generalization: Limited validation across different mIF platforms (e.g., Orion)
  5. Not a replacement: ROSIE cannot replace full CODEX experiment for all biomarkers

Clinical Applications

  1. Screening tool: Prioritize patients for expensive immunostaining
  2. Cost reduction: Reduce need for mIF assays
  3. Faster turnaround: Predictions from existing H&E slides
  4. Resource-limited settings: Democratize biomarker access
