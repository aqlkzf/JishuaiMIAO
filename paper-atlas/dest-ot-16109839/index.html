DeST-OT: Alignment of Spatiotemporal Transcriptomics Data

Open paper

DeST-OT：从两张时空转录组切片推断祖先、后代与生长

1. 它真正要解决的问题

DeST-OT 的输入是同一发育过程在两个时间点采集的空间转录组切片。第一张切片有 $n_1$ 个 spot，第二张有 $n_2$ 个 spot；每个 spot 同时具有基因表达和二维空间坐标。方法输出：

  1. 对齐矩阵 $\Pi\in\mathbb{R}+^{n_1\times n_2}$，其中 $\Pi{ij}$ 表示早期 spot $i$ 向晚期 spot $j$ 输送的质量；
  2. 生长向量 $\xi=\Pi\mathbf 1_{n_2}-\mathbf g_1$，描述早期每个 spot 相对均匀初始质量的增减；
  3. 可选的连续时间生长率 $J_i$。

这里的“祖先—后代”是最优传输意义下的概率性对应，不是实验谱系追踪。

2. 为什么采用半松弛最优传输

发育关系具有时间方向：晚期出现的每个 spot 都应被解释，但早期 spot 可以增殖、死亡或离开观测区域。DeST-OT 因而固定晚期边缘分布：

\[\Pi^T\mathbf 1_{n_1}=\mathbf g_2,\]

同时只用 KL 惩罚约束早期边缘 $\Pi\mathbf 1_{n_2}$ 接近均匀分布 $\mathbf g_1$。这介于平衡 OT 和完全非平衡 OT 之间。

代码中的半松弛标定尤其重要：

\[\mathbf g_1=\frac{1}{n_1}\mathbf 1_{n_1},\qquad \mathbf g_2=\frac{1}{n_1}\mathbf 1_{n_2}.\]

所以总传输质量是 $n_2/n_1$，而不是 1。这使 spot 数量变化直接进入质量预算。实现位于 DeST_OT/src/destot/destot_opt.py:101-117。

3. 从 AnnData 到五个距离矩阵

align() 先取两个切片的共同基因，再将两者拼接后共同执行 normalize_total、log1p 和 30 维 PCA（DESTOT.py:116-130）。随后构造：

  • $C$：跨切片 PCA 欧氏距离，形状 $n_1\times n_2$；
  • $C^{(1)},C^{(2)}$：各切片内部的 PCA 距离；
  • $D^{(1)},D^{(2)}$：各切片内部的空间坐标距离。

当 spatial=False 时，$D^{(1)}$ 和 $D^{(2)}$ 被替换为全 1 矩阵，等价于只保留表达几何（DESTOT.py:132-142）。

4. 双元、三元与四元关系

4.1 双元项

\[\mathcal E_{\mathrm{doublet}}(\Pi)=\langle C,\Pi\rangle_F.\]

它鼓励表达相近的早期和晚期 spot 获得较大传输质量。

4.2 合并的表达—空间几何

\[M^{(k)}=C^{(k)}\odot D^{(k)},\quad k\in\{1,2\}.\]

代码确实使用逐元素乘法 M1 = D1 * C1、M2 = D2 * C2（destot_opt.py:78-90）。$M$ 不是把空间坐标和表达向量拼接，也不是普通加权和。任一距离接近零都会压低乘积，因此不应把它过度简化为“同时接近才接近”。

4.3 三元项

三元项考察“一个祖先对应多个后代”或“多个祖先对应一个后代”时，同侧 spot 在合并几何中是否相近。实现梯度为：

\[r_1(M^{(1)}\odot M^{(1)})\Pi+r_2\Pi(M^{(2)}\odot M^{(2)}),\]

对应 destot_opt.py:135-136。这里的平方是逐元素平方，不是矩阵平方。

4.4 四元项

四元项是基于 $M^{(1)},M^{(2)}$ 的 Gromov–Wasserstein 型项，比较早期 spot 对与晚期 spot 对的内部距离。半松弛版本的梯度比平衡 GW 多一项，因为早期边缘不是固定常数；代码在 destot_opt.py:138-141 分别实现两种分支。

4.5 总目标

\[\min_{\Pi\ge0}(1-\alpha)\mathcal E_{\mathrm{doublet}}+\alpha[\beta\mathcal E_{\mathrm{triplet}}+(1-\beta)\mathcal E_{\mathrm{quartet}}]+\gamma\,\mathrm{KL}(\Pi\mathbf1\|\mathbf g_1)-\varepsilon H(\Pi),\]

并满足 $\Pi^T\mathbf1=\mathbf g_2$。$\alpha$ 平衡跨时间表达匹配与切片内几何，$\beta$ 平衡三元与四元项，$\gamma$ 控制早期边缘偏离均匀分布的程度，$\varepsilon$ 控制熵平滑。代码默认值为 $\alpha=0.2,\beta=0.5,\gamma=50,\varepsilon=0.1$（DESTOT.py:72-76）。

5. 代码怎样求解

LogSinkhorn_iteration() 每轮先用当前 $\Pi_k$ 计算三元与四元梯度，再组合为

\[G_k=(1-\alpha)C+\alpha[\beta G_{\mathrm{triplet}}+(1-\beta)G_{\mathrm{quartet}}].\]

随后在 log 域更新两个对偶变量。早期边缘对应的更新乘以 $\gamma/(\gamma+\varepsilon)$，晚期边缘则使用标准 Sinkhorn 更新，从而实现“一侧软约束、一侧硬约束”（destot_opt.py:126-153）。每轮都计算 stationarity gap，但 while k < max_iter 没有按 gap 提前停止。

一个重要的纸—码差异是复杂度。论文把 Sinkhorn 核乘法概括为每轮 $O(n_1n_2)$；当前实现还会显式计算 $M_1^2\Pi$、$\Pi M_2^2$ 和 GW 型矩阵乘法，因此实际每轮主要是 $O(n_1^2n_2+n_1n_2^2)$。

另一个实现细节是 destot_opt.py:95 中 N1**1/2 按 Python 运算优先级等于 N1/2，不是 $\sqrt{N_1}$。因此把 r2 写成平方根缩放会与实际代码不符。

6. 如何从传输质量读出生长

求解后 $\xi=\Pi\mathbf1_{n_2}-\mathbf g_1$。若 normalize_xi=True，align() 再乘以 $n_1$，于是 $\xi_i=1$ 可读作相对一个初始 spot 多出一个 spot 的质量（DESTOT.py:164-168）。已归一化时的连续生长率为

\[J_i=\frac{\log(1+\xi_i)}{t_2-t_1},\]

未归一化时则在对数内使用 $1+n_1\xi_i$（DESTOT.py:49-70）。若数值使对数参数不为正，代码没有额外保护。

7. 评价量回答什么

  • 生长失真：比较同一细胞类型内推断的 $\xi$ 与该类型期望质量变化；它衡量类型内一致性，不是独立的真实谱系验证。
  • 迁移距离：先通过 $\Pi$ 加权的 Procrustes 刚体配准，再计算期望空间距离（metrics.py:7-36,181-213）。若真实组织经历非刚性形变，较低迁移值不必然等于更正确。
  • 转移矩阵：transition_mat() 汇总细胞类型块中的传输质量，默认按列归一化（metrics.py:39-79）。

10. 一句话总结

DeST-OT 的核心不是“把两张空间图刚性叠起来”，而是在晚期质量必须被解释、早期质量允许增减的约束下，同时权衡跨时间表达相似、分叉/汇聚结构与切片内表达—空间几何。

DeST-OT: Alignment of Spatiotemporal Transcriptomics Data

Citation

Halmos P, Liu X, Gold J, Chen F, Ding L, Raphael BJ. DeST-OT: Alignment of spatiotemporal transcriptomics data. Cell Systems. 2025;16(1). DOI: 10.1016/j.cels.2024.12.001

One-Sentence Summary

DeST-OT uses semi-relaxed optimal transport with a development-aware objective (doublet + triplet + quartet terms over merged feature-spatial matrices) to align spatial transcriptomics slices across time points, enabling unsupervised inference of cell-specific growth/death rates and cell-type transition dynamics.

Problem Statement

Given two spatially resolved transcriptomics (SRT) slices $S_1 = (\mathbf{X}^{(1)}, \mathbf{S}^{(1)})$ and $S_2 = (\mathbf{X}^{(2)}, \mathbf{S}^{(2)})$ from developmental time points $t_1 < t_2$, find an alignment matrix $\Pi \in \mathbb{R}^{n_1 \times n_2}+$ where $\Pi{ij’}$ gives the probability that cell $i$ at $t_1$ is the ancestor of cell $j’$ at $t_2$. The alignment must account for:

  • Cell growth (one ancestor → multiple descendants)
  • Cell death (ancestor → no descendants)
  • Cell-type transitions during development
  • Joint spatial and transcriptomic similarity

Key Contributions

  1. Semi-relaxed OT framework: Relaxes only the first marginal ($\Pi \mathbf{1}{n_2}$ free, $\Pi^T \mathbf{1}{n_1} = \mathbf{g}_2$ fixed), ensuring all descendant cells are mapped while allowing growth/death inference without prior knowledge
  2. Development-aware objective: Three-term cost combining doublet (cell-cell expression), triplet (ancestor-descendant bifurcation), and quartet (geometric consistency via Gromov-Wasserstein)
  3. Merged feature-spatial matrices: $\mathbf{M}^{(i)} = \mathbf{C}^{(i)} \odot \mathbf{D}^{(i)}$ (element-wise product of intra-slice feature and spatial distances) replacing rigid spatial distances
  4. Growth distortion metric: Novel evaluation metric quantifying how well inferred growth matches observed cell-type proportion changes
  5. Migration metric: Quantifies distance cells travel under alignment (with Procrustes registration)

Algorithm Overview

Optimization Problem (Equation 14)

\(\min_{\Pi} (1-\alpha)\mathcal{E}_{\text{doublet}}(\Pi) + \alpha[\beta\mathcal{E}_{\text{triplet}}(\Pi) + (1-\beta)\mathcal{E}_{\text{quartet}}(\Pi)] + \gamma \text{KL}(\Pi\mathbf{1}_{n_2} \| \mathbf{g}_1) - \varepsilon H(\Pi)\) \(\text{s.t.} \quad \Pi^T \mathbf{1}_{n_1} = \mathbf{g}_2, \quad \Pi \geq 0\)

Three Objective Terms

| Term | Formula | What it models | |——|———|—————-| | Doublet | $\sum_{i,j’} C_{ij’}\Pi_{ij’}$ | Cells aligned together should have similar gene expression | | Triplet (Eq. 9) | $\sum_{ij’k’} \Pi_{ij’}\Pi_{ik’}M_{j’k’}^{(2)2} + \sum_{ijk’}\Pi_{ik’}\Pi_{jk’}M_{ij}^{(1)2}$ | Descendants of same ancestor should be close; multiple ancestors of same descendant should be close | | Quartet (Eq. 8) | $\sum_{i,j’,k,l’}(M_{ik}^{(1)} - M_{j’l’}^{(2)})^2\Pi_{ij’}\Pi_{kl’}$ | Merged feature-spatial geometry should be preserved across time |

Solver

  • Log-domain semi-relaxed Sinkhorn algorithm
  • Unbalanced coefficient: $\text{ubc} = \gamma/(\gamma + \varepsilon)$ applied to the first dual variable (f) only
  • Gradient per iteration: $(1-\alpha)C + \alpha[\beta \cdot \text{dist_orig} + (1-\beta) \cdot \text{dist_GW}]$
  • Convergence checked via Frobenius norm of $\Pi_{k} - \Pi_{k-1}$

Outputs

  1. Alignment matrix $\Pi \in \mathbb{R}^{n_1 \times n_2}$: probabilistic ancestor-descendant mapping
  2. Growth vector $\xi = \Pi\mathbf{1}_{n_2} - \mathbf{g}_1$: per-cell mass-flux
  3. Growth rate $J_i = \log(1 + n_1\xi_i)/(t_2 - t_1)$: continuous-time growth rate

Hyperparameters

Parameter Default Description
$\alpha$ 0.2 Balance between doublet and merged feature-spatial terms
$\beta$ 0.5 Balance between triplet and quartet within merged term
$\gamma$ 50 KL penalty strength on first marginal
$\varepsilon$ 0.1 Entropy regularization strength
max_iter 100 Maximum Sinkhorn iterations
balanced False Semi-relaxed (False) vs balanced (True) OT

Cutoff-robust setting: For tissues exceeding capture area, use $\alpha = 0.99$, $\gamma = 1$.

Key Results

Simulated Data

  • 1D slices (51 spots, 2 cell types): DeST-OT alignments robust to all $\alpha$ values; moscot fails at high $\alpha$ because its GW term prefers shape-matching
  • 2D slices (988 spots, Gaussian noise $\sigma = 0.1$–$0.8$): DeST-OT correctly captures cell growth/death across noise levels; consistently lower growth distortion than moscot

Mouse Kidney (Visium, 4 time points)

  • DeST-OT achieves best trade-off between low growth distortion and low migration across all 6 slice pairs
  • DeST-OT growth rates identify differential growth patterns (renal cortex, calyx, medulla) with biologically consistent, region-homogeneous patterns
  • moscot growth patterns are sparse (computational artifact of unbalanced OT)

Growth Rate Correlation (Mouse Kidney)

  • DeST-OT growth rates show much stronger Spearman correlation with annotated growth genes (e.g., Ube2c: $\rho = -0.423$) and apoptosis genes (e.g., Gm2a: $\rho = -0.579$) than moscot
  • DeST-OT identifies 27–60 significant growth genes per pair vs 6–39 for moscot (FDR < 0.05)

Axolotl Brain (Stereo-seq, 5 stages, up to 11,698 cells)

  • Lowest growth distortion among all methods while maintaining low migration
  • Cell-type transition graph recovers known developmental trajectories (e.g., dEGCs → 11 descendant types, immature → mature transitions)
  • Spectral co-clustering of DeST-OT alignment matrix identifies cell types with differential growth patterns (highest AMI with annotations)

Computational Complexity

  • Paper claim: $O(n_1 n_2)$ per Sinkhorn iteration.
  • Released implementation: explicit products such as $M_1^2\Pi$ and $\Pi M_2^2$ make the dense per-iteration cost $O(n_1^2n_2+n_1n_2^2)$; this is a material paper-code boundary.
  • Space: $O(n_1^2 + 2n_1 n_2 + n_2^2)$ for distance matrices
  • Runs in seconds on axolotl dataset (up to 11,698 cells)

Limitations

  1. Scalability to datasets with >10K cells (authors suggest low-rank OT approximations)
  2. No continuous-time dynamics modeling (operates on discrete time point pairs)
  3. Growth rate conversion assumes operator splitting (decoupled growth/transport)
  4. Requires common gene set between slices (intersects var.index)
  5. PCA dimension (30 components) and preprocessing (normalize_total + log1p) are hardcoded
  6. No ground-truth validation of spatiotemporal alignments beyond metrics
  7. Migration metric assumes rigid-body transformation (may not capture non-rigid deformations)
