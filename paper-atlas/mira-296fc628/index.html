MIRA

MIRA: joint regulatory modeling of multimodal expression and chromatin accessibility in single cells

Open paper Code

MIRA 方法中文解读

MIRA 要解决的问题是：同一个单细胞里同时测到 RNA 表达和染色质可及性后，研究者不只想把细胞画到一个共同 UMAP 上，还想知道发育分支点由哪些调控因子推动，以及某个基因的表达到底是否主要由其附近染色质开放解释。论文明确指出，已有 joint analysis 方法能把两种模态投影到共享潜空间，但缺少系统对比转录和可及性的工具来解释发育命运决定中的调控回路 (paper source/nature_html/paper.md:21-27)。

MIRA 的核心可以分成两层。

同一细胞的 RNA count + ATAC peak matrix
        |
        v
表达 topic model + 可及性 topic model
        |
        v
topic composition -> ILR 变换 -> 拼接成 joint representation
        |
        v
KNN 图 -> pseudotime/transport map -> terminal probability -> cell-state tree
        |
        v
沿树比较 expression topics 和 accessibility topics
        |
        v
每个基因的 RP 模型
  LITE: 只用 TSS 附近局部可及性
  NITE: 局部可及性 + 全局 accessibility topics
        |
        v
NITE score、chromatin differential、候选 driver TF

第一层是细胞级 topic model。MIRA 分别对 RNA 和 ATAC 建模：RNA 计数用 negative binomial，ATAC peak 可及性用 multinomial/稀疏 peak 分布；每个细胞由少量 topic 的 composition 表示，每个 topic 对应一组共表达基因或共可及区域 (paper source/nature_html/paper.md:219-270)。代码中对应 ExpressionTopicModel 和 AccessibilityTopicModel，其 Pyro 生成模型分别实现 RNA 的 negative-binomial likelihood 和 ATAC 的 peak likelihood (MIRA/mira/topic_model/model_factory.py:21-64, MIRA/mira/topic_model/generative_models/lda_generative.py:90-195)。

第二步是把两个模态拼成 joint representation。论文先取 topic posterior expectation，然后用 ILR 变换把 simplex 上的 topic composition 投影到实数空间，再把 RNA 和 ATAC 的向量拼接为 J_i (paper source/nature_html/paper.md:291-312)。代码里 get_umap_features / get_hierarchical_umap_features 做 topic 特征变换，make_joint_representation 取两个 AnnData 的共同细胞并水平拼接 .obsm 特征，写入 X_joint_umap_features (MIRA/mira/topic_model/base.py:1392-1483, MIRA/mira/adata_interface/utils.py:40-104)。

第三步是 trajectory 和树结构。MIRA 在 joint space 上构建 KNN 图，用 Palantir 思路得到 pseudotime 和 forward Markov transport map，再计算每个细胞到不同 terminal state 的吸收概率。论文将分支点定义为两个 terminal probability ratio 相对起点发生显著分化的最早 pseudotime (paper source/nature_html/paper.md:315-348)。代码中 get_transport_map 负责 pseudotime、边修剪和 Markov 矩阵，get_branch_probabilities 用吸收 Markov chain 计算 terminal probabilities，get_tree_structure 反复合并最后分叉的 lineage 来生成树 (MIRA/mira/pseudotime/pseudotime.py:212-400, MIRA/mira/pseudotime/pseudotime.py:482-754)。

第四步是基因级 regulatory potential 模型。对每个基因，MIRA 在 TSS 附近划分 upstream、downstream 和 promoter 区域，让 peak 的影响随距离按 learned half-decay distance 衰减，然后用这些局部可及性预测该基因表达 (paper source/nature_html/paper.md:351-379)。代码中 GeneModel.RP 正是 weights * 0.5 ** distance 型距离衰减求和，GeneModel.model 将 upstream、downstream、promoter 三部分加权后进入 negative-binomial 表达 likelihood (MIRA/mira/rp_model/rp_model.py:840-941)。

LITE 和 NITE 是 MIRA 最有解释力的部分。LITE 模型只问“局部 chromatin accessibility 是否足够解释表达”；NITE 模型在 LITE 的基础上加入全局 accessibility topics，表示基因表达可能受更大尺度的细胞状态或非局部信号影响 (paper source/nature_html/paper.md:381-390)。代码用同一个 GeneModel 的 use_NITE_features 开关实现 NITE：打开后会学习 topic 系数并把 NITE_features @ a_NITE 加到 RP predictor 上 (MIRA/mira/rp_model/rp_model.py:845-928)。

NITE score 是 LITE 与 NITE likelihood 的比值检验。若 NITE 明显比 LITE 更能解释表达，则说明该基因不是单靠局部可及性调控。论文还定义了 cell-level NITE score 和 chromatin differential，用来显示局部 chromatin 在某个细胞对某个基因表达的高估或低估 (paper source/nature_html/paper.md:393-441)。代码中 _get_NITE_score 计算 -2 * (LITE_logp - NITE_logp) 并按非零 count 归一化，get_chromatin_differential 计算 log2(LITE prediction) - log2(NITE prediction) (MIRA/mira/tools/lite_nite.py:7-148)。

论文的关键生物发现是：早期发育基因更常是 LITE，即表达和局部 chromatin opening 同步；终末命运或终末功能基因更常是 NITE，即局部 accessibility 可能已经打开，但真正表达还需要命运信号或转录因子输入 (paper source/nature_html/paper.md:193-198)。在毛囊例子中，Krt23 的局部可及性不能很好预测表达，而 NITE 模型和 medulla accessibility topic a5 更吻合；分支 primed genes 则在分支前可及性升高，之后只在某个 lineage 中表达，候选 driver 包括 Smad5/Smad2/3 和 Rbpj (paper source/nature_html/paper.md:85-118)。

核心算法代码与论文高度一致，包括 topic model、joint representation、trajectory tree、RP/LITE/NITE、chromatin differential、motif/pISD driver test。但完整图表复现还需要论文外部提供的数据、benchmark 资源和 notebooks；这些不是当前源码仓库的一部分 (paper source/nature_html/paper.md:456-460)。

MIRA Summary

MIRA is a multimodal single-cell regulatory analysis method for matched scRNA-seq and scATAC-seq cells. The paper argues that prior joint-embedding methods can visualize or cluster multimodal cells, but do not rigorously contrast expression and accessibility to explain fate decisions and gene-level regulatory logic (paper source/nature_html/paper.md:21-27).

The method has two coupled layers. First, separate probabilistic topic models learn sparse expression and accessibility topics, transform topic compositions with an ILR transform, and concatenate RNA and ATAC features into a joint representation used for KNN graphs, UMAP, clustering, pseudotime, and cell-state tree parsing (paper source/nature_html/paper.md:47-56, paper source/nature_html/paper.md:291-312). Second, gene-level regulatory-potential models compare a local chromatin model, LITE, with an expanded NITE model that also uses genome-wide accessibility topics (paper source/nature_html/paper.md:351-390).

The paper’s main evaluation uses synthetic trajectory scaffolds and biological differentiation datasets. MIRA outperformed PCA/LSI/Slingshot alternatives on the geometric mean of edge accuracy, branch F1 score, and pseudotime correlation across scaffold difficulty and read depths (paper source/nature_html/paper.md:44-50). In hair follicle and embryonic brain examples, topic dynamics and NITE scores distinguish early genes whose expression tracks local chromatin accessibility from terminal fate/function genes whose expression is better explained by nonlocal accessibility-state features (paper source/nature_html/paper.md:82-118, paper source/nature_html/paper.md:180-204).

The official code repository is available and cloned locally at MIRA/ from https://github.com/cistrome/MIRA (paper source/nature_html/paper.md:456-460). Code-paper fidelity is high for the core package: topic models, joint representation, pseudotime/tree parsing, RP modeling, LITE/NITE scores, chromatin differential, motif scanning, and pISD driver testing are implemented in the package. Exact benchmark and figure reproduction is partial because the cloned package does not include all paper-specific benchmark scripts, processed datasets, or notebooks.

Main limitations are interpretive rather than purely computational. The authors explicitly warn that single-cell trajectories are not clonal lineage tracing, can colocate convergent cell states, and depend on origin choice, biological priors, and data resolution (paper source/nature_html/paper.md:201-201).
