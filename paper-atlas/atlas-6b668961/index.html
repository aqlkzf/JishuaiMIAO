ATLAS 方法中文解读

它解决什么问题？

神经环路既需要知道“谁给目标细胞输入”，也需要知道“目标细胞把信息传给谁”。改造狂犬病毒擅长单突触逆行示踪，即从起始细胞找到其上游输入；但缺少一个同等级、能从遗传学指定的起始细胞向下游进行单突触顺行示踪的工具。AAV1、黄热病毒等曾被用于顺行转神经元标记，但方向特异性、严格突触依赖性或起始细胞遗传选择能力不足。

ATLAS（anterograde transsynaptic label based on antibody-like sensors）把一次正常的突触囊泡释放改造成“递送重组酶”的过程，使下游细胞永久开启荧光报告基因。

核心构造

VAMP2 — ALFA标签 — BACE切割位点 — AMPA.FingR — Cre/FLP
  • VAMP2 把融合蛋白装入突触前囊泡。
  • BACE 切割位点使后半段载荷与膜锚定部分分离。
  • AMPA.FingR（AF）是经 mRNA display 筛选得到的纤维连接蛋白结构域，结合突触后 GluA1 的胞外 N 端。
  • Cre 或 FLP 进入突触后细胞核，开启对应的 DIO/fDIO 报告基因。
  • ALFA 标签用于识别表达 ATLAS 的起始细胞及其轴突终末。

从输入到输出

选择起始细胞类型与预期下游脑区
        ↓
在起始细胞表达 ATLAS；在下游脑区注入重组酶依赖报告病毒
        ↓
ATLAS 经 VAMP2 进入突触囊泡，BACE 切下载荷
        ↓
神经活动触发囊泡胞吐，AF-重组酶进入突触间隙
        ↓
AF 结合突触后 GluA1，并经内吞进入细胞
        ↓
重组酶到达细胞核，激活荧光报告基因
        ↓
获得可观察、可进一步操控的下游神经元

如果需要限定起始细胞类型，可使用 DIO-ATLAS~FLP~：Cre 决定哪些起始细胞表达 ATLAS，而被运输的 FLP 在下游细胞中打开 fDIO 报告基因，避免输入选择和输出读取使用同一种重组酶。

关键实验结果

AF 在培养神经元中定位于突触后和网格蛋白相关区域，并能被内吞；网格蛋白抑制剂 chlorpromazine 会降低摄取。含 AF 的 pHluorin 载荷释放后荧光持续时间明显延长，KCl 去极化使释放事件约增加 15 倍，说明递送与神经活动和突触囊泡胞吐相关。

体内实验覆盖 mPFC→纹状体、V1→上丘、dLGN→V1、运动皮层→纹状体/丘脑，以及大鼠腹侧 CA1→伏隔核等通路。功能验证中，10/10 个 ATLAS 标记的纹状体细胞与表达 ChR2 的 V1 输入相连；邻近未标记细胞为 7/10，随机未标记细胞为 7/17。

方向与级数验证也很重要：反向注射没有产生明显上游标记；V1→上丘→PBG 的间接路径不标记 PBG，而直接在上丘表达 ATLAS 可以标记 PBG，支持在这些实验条件下的严格顺行和单突触性质。

效率、毒性与可调参数

内源 BACE 可能不足。mPFC→纹状体实验中，ATLAS 单独使用标记 144 ± 17 个细胞；BACE:ATLAS 为 5:1 时提高到 286 ± 17 个，约覆盖 10% 的突触后神经元。完整 ATLAS 长期表达可出现退变突起和肿胀胞体；缩短版本 ATLASsn 在论文测试中显著降低毒性，因此更适合较长周期实验。

如何正确理解结果

ATLAS 标记意味着该细胞满足一整串条件：起始细胞表达、囊泡装载与切割、活动依赖释放、GluA1 结合和内吞、核转运以及报告基因重组。它不是突触强度的定量读数，也不是 100% 灵敏的连接组测量。当前 AF 只针对兴奋性突触的 GluA1；研究者通常还必须预先知道大致下游区域并注入报告病毒。

论文提供 Addgene 质粒（232351–232353）、G-Node 定量图像和源数据，但没有可执行软件仓库。跨脑区的统一假阳性率和假阴性率未给出。因此，ATLAS 更适合被看作一个可模块化演化的遗传通路访问平台，而不是开箱即用的全脑连接组方案。

ATLAS: rationally designed anterograde transsynaptic tracing

Overview

ATLAS is a modular protein tracer that gives genetic access to neurons postsynaptic to a chosen starter population. It addresses a major directional gap in circuit mapping: modified rabies virus is powerful for monosynaptic retrograde tracing, while earlier anterograde agents such as AAV1 and yellow fever virus were not comparable tools for tracing from genetically determined cells and can show transport outside the desired direction.

The construct couples five functions in one chain: VAMP2 targets synaptic vesicles; a BACE1 cleavage site releases the payload; AMPA.FingR binds the extracellular N terminus of postsynaptic GluA1; Cre or FLP activates a reporter; and a nuclear-localization domain helps the recombinase reach the nucleus. A Cre-dependent DIO-ATLAS~FLP~ configuration restricts starter cells genetically while retaining a distinct FLP output in downstream cells.

Main evidence

Mechanistic experiments show AF binding near postsynaptic sites, clathrin-associated endocytosis, activity-enhanced synaptic-vesicle release, and reporter recombination in ALFA-negative postsynaptic cells. In vivo, ATLAS traced multiple known excitatory pathways, including mPFC→striatum, V1→superior colliculus, dLGN→V1, motor cortex→striatum/thalamus, and rat ventral CA1→nucleus accumbens.

Specificity tests support the intended behavior in the studied circuits. Reporter labeling was absent or near control levels in inhibitory-output and reverse-direction tests. An indirect V1→SC→PBG arrangement did not label PBG, whereas direct SC expression did, supporting monosynaptic restriction. In functional testing, 10/10 ATLAS-labeled striatal neurons were connected to optogenetically stimulated V1 inputs, compared with 7/10 adjacent unlabeled and 7/17 random unlabeled cells.

Efficiency is tunable but incomplete. In the mPFC→striatum assay, ATLAS alone labeled 144 ± 17 cells; a 5:1 BACE:ATLAS ratio increased this to 286 ± 17 cells, approximately 10% of postsynaptic neurons. The shortened ATLASsn variant reduces the toxicity observed after longer expression of full-length ATLAS. Activity dependence creates potential for labeling behaviorally engaged circuits, although temporal gating is proposed rather than demonstrated here.

Strengths and limitations

The central strength is mechanistic design: release, receptor binding, uptake and reporter activation are assigned to replaceable protein modules. The current AF module is nevertheless restricted to GluA1-containing excitatory synapses. Target regions usually need prior identification and delivery of a recombinase-dependent reporter. Labeling is not fully sensitive, BACE supplementation adds experimental complexity, and cross-circuit false-positive/false-negative rates are not established.

Reproducibility

This is a paper-only analysis. No executable implementation repository was found in the article or acquisition sidecars. The paper provides detailed construct sequences and experimental methods; ATLASsn~Cre~, DIO-ATLASsn~Flp~ and DIO-BACE-HA plasmids are available through Addgene (IDs 232351–232353), quantified images are deposited on G-Node, and source data accompany the article. Reproduction therefore depends on wet-lab capability, viral production, regional targeting and reporter design rather than software. Reproducibility assessment: 3/5—key materials and extensive protocols are available, but complete raw microscopy requires request and the system has substantial circuit- and parameter-dependent experimental choices.
