## CCLA 方法解释

### 论文解决什么问题

这篇 Nature Communications 2023 论文研究一个常被忽略的问题：常用癌症细胞系是否真的足够同质，还是内部也存在会影响实验解释的转录和表观遗传异质性。作者构建 CCLA 资源，对 42 个细胞系做 scRNA-seq，对 39 个细胞系做 scATAC-seq，并进一步分析 CNV、染色质可及性、ecDNA、谱系追踪和缺氧刺激如何影响细胞系内部状态 (`paper.md:12-24`)。

### 为什么已有资源不够

过去大量单细胞研究集中在临床肿瘤样本，细胞系常被当成相对稳定、均一的模型。但论文指出，细胞系可能演化出不同 strain，也可能在 EMT、耐药或应激反应中出现亚群差异；只用 bulk 数据或单一转录组视角，很难解释这些差异的来源 (`paper.md:18-24`)。scRNA-seq 能看见表达状态，但不能直接解释调控来源；scATAC-seq 则能提供增强子、启动子和 TF 活性相关证据 (`paper.md:21-21`)。

### CCLA 的核心流程

```text
癌症细胞系面板
  |
  |-- scRNA-seq
  |     |-- pooled sequencing 后按表达特征分配细胞系
  |     |-- Seurat/UMAP 构建细胞系图谱
  |     |-- PCA 质心距离计算 diversity score
  |     |-- 每个细胞系做 NMF，提取共同异质表达程序
  |     |-- inferCNV 分析 CNV subclone 与转录亚群关系
  |
  |-- scATAC-seq
  |     |-- ArchR/LSI/UMAP 构建染色质可及性图谱
  |     |-- motif/ChromVAR/SCENIC 推断 TF 活性
  |     |-- 与 scRNA-seq label transfer，匹配转录亚群和 ATAC 亚群
  |     |-- 100 kb bin coverage -> ecDNA candidate -> TSS/cell-count filter -> 合并 ecDNA region
  |
  |-- 动态实验
        |-- Cell-ID barcode 谱系追踪
        |-- 缺氧处理前后 Seurat integration 和 TF 解释
```

### 关键计算步骤

第一步是构建 scRNA 图谱。论文把多个细胞系混合上机，再根据表达特征分配回对应细胞系；最终得到 42 个细胞系的转录图谱，并用 CCLE/GEO bulk RNA-seq 做分配验证 (`paper.md:30-44`)。代码中的 `scRNA_atalas.R` 支持 Seurat UMAP、UMI/gene/cell 数量 QC、batch/library/cancer-type 可视化 (`CCLA_repo/scripts/scRNA_atalas.R:1-94`)。

第二步是量化转录异质性。论文把细胞投影到前 30 个 PC，计算每个细胞到其细胞系质心的平均距离作为 diversity score，并去除均值上下 3 个标准差之外的极端值 (`paper.md:279-282`)。这一步在代码中有直接实现：读取 PCA embedding、保留 1-30 维、按 cell line 计算质心距离 (`CCLA_repo/scripts/scRNA_Diversity_score.R:7-31`)。

第三步是 scATAC 图谱。论文用 ArchR 做 QC、doublet 过滤、LSI/SVD 降维、聚类、gene score 和 motif 分析 (`paper.md:291-306`)。代码 `scATAC_atalas.R` 覆盖了 ArchR hg38 设置、Arrow 文件读取、doublet 过滤、IterativeLSI、UMAP 和 fragment/TSS QC 图 (`CCLA_repo/scripts/scATAC_atalas.R:1-70`)。

第四步是解释异质性的来源。CNV 部分使用 inferCNV，以 RPE-1 和 HK2 作为正常参考，参数包括 cutoff 0.1、400 gene window、denoise 和 HMM (`paper.md:333-336`)；代码 `inferCNV.R` 与这一描述吻合 (`CCLA_repo/scripts/inferCNV.R:1-46`)。TF 部分结合 ArchR/ChromVAR motif 结果和 SCENIC/pySCENIC regulon，把 FOXA2、NFE2L2、NFYB 等 TF 与具体亚群状态联系起来 (`paper.md:107-121`, `paper.md:321-324`)。

第五步是 ecDNA 检测。论文把全基因组分成 100 kb bin，计算每个细胞内 bin coverage，并用相对覆盖度、超过 15 个细胞支持、TSS enrichment/proportion 过滤和片段相关性合并来定义候选 ecDNA 区域 (`paper.md:348-351`)。代码中能找到对应片段：100 kb coverage 计算 (`CCLA_repo/ecDNA_0.1/01.whole_chrom_bin.py:5-56`)、超过 15 个细胞的候选 bin 过滤 (`CCLA_repo/ecDNA_0.1/03.candidate_cell_density.R:6-23`)、TSS proportion 过滤 (`CCLA_repo/ecDNA_0.1/07.tss_filtered.R:13-24`) 和基于相关性的片段合并 (`CCLA_repo/ecDNA_0.1/10.ecDNA.merge.R:40-84`)。

### 主要发现

CCLA 显示，癌症细胞系内部异质性很常见，并不只来自一种机制。CNV 可以解释一部分离散转录亚群，但并不能解释所有离散状态 (`paper.md:90-101`)。scATAC 数据显示，某些转录亚群有对应的染色质可及性和 TF 活性差异，例如 MDA-MB-231 中 FOXA2 相关的 KRAS signaling up 状态 (`paper.md:104-121`)。ecDNA 可能通过改变局部基因拷贝数和表达影响亚群形成，但作者也明确指出，基于 scATAC 的 ecDNA 候选区域可能比 bulk ecDNA 方法有更多假阳性，需要后续验证 (`paper.md:192-192`)。

动态实验说明异质性不是静态标签。Cell-ID barcode 追踪显示，同一祖细胞后代可以在不同时间进入不同转录状态 (`paper.md:141-153`)；缺氧处理则显示，同一个原始亚群内的细胞可能产生不同应激反应，且这种差异可由预先存在的染色质状态和 TF 活性解释 (`paper.md:155-177`)。

### 代码和复现性

论文公开了 CCLA 数据浏览器、CNSA/GSA 数据入口和 GitHub 代码 (`paper.md:381-387`)。但代码仓库更像项目分析脚本集合，而不是一键复现的软件包。很多脚本有绝对路径，缺少环境文件和统一运行入口；NMF 程序发现、完整 pooled-cell assignment、网站构建和部分补充数据生成脚本在本次获得的快照中没有找到。因此，代码适合用来核对关键计算步骤，但不足以直接完整复现整篇论文。

## CCLA Summary

This Nature Communications 2023 paper builds CCLA, a single-cell multi-omics resource for human cancer cell lines, to test whether commonly used in vitro models contain intra-cell-line heterogeneity that is invisible to bulk assays. The study profiles 42 cell lines by scRNA-seq and 39 by scATAC-seq, then asks which transcriptomic, genetic, epigenetic, and ecDNA mechanisms explain heterogeneity within individual lines (`paper.md:12-24`).

The main contribution is an atlas plus analysis workflow rather than a new package. Cells are pooled for sequencing and computationally assigned back to cell lines; the scRNA atlas captures 23,089 cells with reported average 513 cells per line, 34,641 UMI, and 5859 genes per cell (`paper.md:30-44`). The scATAC atlas captures 54,597 high-quality nuclei and uses ArchR-based dimensionality reduction and clustering to assess chromatin heterogeneity (`paper.md:73-87`).

The paper quantifies heterogeneity using discrete/continuous or differential/indiscriminate patterns plus diversity scores. For scRNA, diversity is defined as the average distance from each cell to its cell-line centroid in the top 30 PCs after outlier removal (`paper.md:279-282`), and the released script implements this directly (`CCLA_repo/scripts/scRNA_Diversity_score.R:7-31`). The paper also identifies recurrent NMF expression programs across cancer cell lines, including cell-cycle, EMT, IFN, stress, estrogen-response, mitochondrial, and MYC-related programs (`paper.md:64-68`), although I did not find a standalone NMF script in the released repo.

Mechanistically, the study separates several sources of heterogeneity. inferCNV analysis identifies CNV subclones in 25 of 40 cancer cell lines and links some CNV subclones to transcriptomic subclusters (`paper.md:90-101`); the released inferCNV script matches the paper's RPE-1/HK2 reference setup, cutoff 0.1, 400-gene window, denoising, and HMM settings (`CCLA_repo/scripts/inferCNV.R:1-46`). scATAC/scRNA integration and TF analyses implicate chromatin accessibility and TF programs such as FOXA2, NFE2L2, and NFYB (`paper.md:104-121`), with partial code support in downstream enrichment scripts (`CCLA_repo/scripts/TF_targets_hallmark_dotplot.R:1-52`).

The most method-like part is scATAC-derived ecDNA detection. The paper divides the genome into 100 kb bins, normalizes coverage per cell, keeps high relative-coverage bins present in more than 15 cells, filters by TSS enrichment/proportion, and merges correlated nearby fragments into candidate ecDNA regions (`paper.md:348-351`). The repository contains scripts for bin coverage, candidate support, TSS filtering, and merging (`CCLA_repo/ecDNA_0.1/01.whole_chrom_bin.py:5-56`, `CCLA_repo/ecDNA_0.1/03.candidate_cell_density.R:6-23`, `CCLA_repo/ecDNA_0.1/07.tss_filtered.R:13-24`, `CCLA_repo/ecDNA_0.1/10.ecDNA.merge.R:40-84`). The paper cautions that this scATAC approach may have more false positives than bulk ecDNA methods because junction-read evidence is limited (`paper.md:192-192`).

Reproducibility is medium. Data and the CCLA browser are publicly linked (`paper.md:381-381`), and the paper's custom code is available on GitHub (`paper.md:384-387`). The acquired code snapshot supports specific computations, but it is a set of hard-coded analysis scripts rather than a turnkey workflow: no environment file, command runner, or full NMF/assignment pipeline was found.
