## gsMap 方法中文解读：把 GWAS 遗传信号映射回组织中的空间位置

### 1. gsMap 回答的不是“这是什么细胞”，而是“哪些位置与某个复杂性状相关”

gsMap（genetically informed spatial mapping of cells for complex traits）把空间转录组（ST）与 GWAS summary statistics 连接起来。它对每个 spot 估计一个性状关联显著性，再把这些结果画回组织坐标。高分 spot 表示：与该 spot 特异表达基因相连的 SNP，其 GWAS 信号比基线注释所预期的更强。

这里的 spot 随平台而变：Stereo-seq cell-bin 可以近似单细胞，10x Visium spot 通常包含多个细胞。因此论文所谓“细胞级”分辨率取决于输入平台和上游分割。gsMap 不做细胞分割，也不直接推断因果细胞。

整个计算链可以写成：

$$
(X,\text{coordinates},\text{optional labels})
\rightarrow Z
\rightarrow D_i
\rightarrow S_{ig}
\rightarrow a_{is}
\rightarrow \ell_{is}
\rightarrow P_i
\rightarrow P_{\mathrm{region}}.
$$

其中 $X$ 是表达矩阵，$Z$ 是空间感知嵌入，$D_i$ 是 focal spot $i$ 的微域，$S_{ig}$ 是基因特异性分数（GSS），$a_{is}$ 是 spot 特异的 SNP 注释，$\ell_{is}$ 是 LD score，$P_i$ 和 $P_{\mathrm{region}}$ 分别是 spot 与区域的性状关联 P 值。

### 2. 输入和输出

论文要求四类输入：

1. 含全转录组表达和二维空间坐标的 ST AnnData；
2. GWAS summary statistics；
3. LD reference panel；
4. 可选的 enhancer/表观基因组 SNP-to-gene links。

非人数据还需同源基因表映射到人类基因，因为下游 GWAS 与基因组注释在人类坐标系中。代码的 `run_latent_to_gene()` 会过滤同源表外基因、去重，并在保留不足 100 个基因时停止。

主要输出包括：带 `latent_GVAE`/`latent_PCA` 的 H5AD、gene-by-spot GSS feather、逐染色体/逐 chunk 的 LD score、每个 spot 的 `beta/se/z/p`，以及按组织注释聚合的 Cauchy P 值与空间图。

### 3. 第一步：从表达与坐标学习空间感知嵌入

#### 3.1 表达预处理和空间图

论文将计数按 library size 归一化、log 转换并标准化，保留默认 3,000 个高变基因；可选用 300 个 PCA 成分加速。当前代码在 `preprocess_data()` 中对 count 层运行 Seurat v3 HVG、`normalize_total(target_sum=1e4)` 和 `log1p`，随后在 `LatentRepresentationFinder` 中缩放 HVG 或使用 Pearson residuals。

空间坐标构成 kNN 图。配置默认 `n_neighbors=11`，`cal_spatial_net()` 返回包括自身在内的近邻，再删除距离为 0 的自身边，因此通常留下论文所说的 10 个空间近邻。邻接矩阵随后加 self-loop 并对称归一化。

#### 3.2 GAT auto-encoder

GAT 编码器从表达特征与空间边得到嵌入 $Z$。attention 允许不同空间邻居具有不同权重：空间上相邻但表达不同的 spot 不必被同等聚合，这正是 gsMap 用来减少过度平滑的设计。

训练损失包含表达重建与可选的细胞类型分类：

$$
L=\gamma L_{\mathrm{rec}}+(1-\gamma)L_{\mathrm{label}}.
$$

没有标签时只用重建损失；有标签时，当前代码以 `rec_w` 和 `label_w` 对 MSE 与 cross-entropy 加权。标签只是改善嵌入的先验，不是必需输入。代码会去除缺失标签，并只保留至少 30 个 spot 的标签组。

论文与当前快照存在参数差异：论文描述 $m=32$、最多 1,000 次迭代、weight decay $10^{-4}$；本地默认配置为 `gat_hidden2=30`、`epochs=300`、`gcn_decay=0.01`。因此架构主线是 Exact，默认数值只能标为 Partial。

### 4. 第二步：为每个 focal spot 构造微域

空间邻近不保证同类型，单看表达又容易受 ST 稀疏噪声影响。gsMap 先在较大的空间候选邻域中找候选 spot，再按嵌入余弦相似度排序：

$$
\cos(\theta_{ij})=\frac{Z_i^{\mathsf T}Z_j}{\lVert Z_i\rVert\lVert Z_j\rVert}.
$$

对 focal spot $i$，取最相似的前 $d$ 个 spot 构成微域 $D_i$。论文在 10x Visium 使用 $d=20$，Stereo-seq 使用 $d=50$。当前配置默认 `num_neighbour=21`、空间候选数 `num_neighbour_spatial=101`；由于候选列表包含 focal spot，本质上对应 focal 加约 20 个同质邻居。若提供 annotation，代码只在同一 annotation 内选择邻居。

微域不是新的解剖区域或细胞类型，它只是对 focal spot 的局部、同质表达证据做稳健聚合。

### 5. 第三步：计算 gene specificity score

gsMap 在每个 spot 内把基因按表达从低到高排名。对基因 $g$，比较它在 focal 微域中的几何平均 rank 与整张切片中的几何平均 rank。直观上可写为：

$$
F_{ig}=\frac{\operatorname{GM}_{k\in D_i}(R_{kg})}
{\operatorname{GM}_{j=1}^{n}(R_{jg})}.
$$

若 $F_{ig}\le 1$，该基因在微域内不比全局更特异，置零。代码还比较“微域表达比例 / 全局表达比例”；若不大于 1，也置零，以避免少数极高 rank 离群值制造虚假特异性。

一个小例子：某基因在三点微域的 rank 为 $(90,80,100)$，全切片几何平均 rank 为 20，则局部几何平均约为 89.6，$F\approx4.48$，说明它在该微域相对特异。若该基因只在其中一个 spot 非零，而微域表达比例不高于全局比例，表达比例门控仍会把它清零。

论文给出的最终投影是

$$
S_{ig}=\exp(F_{ig}^{2})-1.
$$

但当前 `latent_to_gene.py` 先将每个 rank 除以全局几何均值，再对微域取几何均值，之后执行 `np.exp(gene_ranks_region)-1`。也就是说，本地快照实现的是对归一化 rank 比值做 $\exp(F)-1$，没有显式平方。这是重要的 `Partial`，不能只按论文公式描述代码。

### 6. 第四步：从基因分数变成 spot 特异 SNP 注释

默认情况下，代码将 SNP 配给基因转录区域上下游各 50 kb 内的候选基因；若一个 SNP 命中多个基因窗口，`overlaps_gtf_bim()` 选择距 TSS 最近者。可选 enhancer 文件扩展 SNP-to-gene 连接，并提供 gene-window-first、enhancer-first 或 enhancer-only 等优先策略。

于是同一个 SNP 在不同 spot 上可以获得不同权重，因为它连接基因的 GSS 随空间位置改变。对每个 spot 都得到一列连续 SNP annotation。随后使用 LD reference 计算

$$
\ell_i(j)=\sum_k r_{jk}^{2}a_i(k),
$$

即 SNP $j$ 与 spot $i$ 注释中其他 SNP 的 LD 加权总和。代码按染色体和 spot chunk 处理，并保留 baseline 与回归权重文件以控制内存和运行时间。

### 7. 第五步：逐 spot 的空间 S-LDSC

gsMap 把每个 spot 当成一个连续功能注释，在 baseline annotations 条件下回归 GWAS $\chi^2$：

$$
E[\chi_j^2]=N\sum_C\tau_C\ell(j,C)+Na+1.
$$

其中焦点系数检验“高 GSS SNP 是否解释了超出基线的遗传力”。当前代码先对 summary statistics 与 LD reference 取共同 SNP，过滤极端 $\chi^2$，以加权最小二乘和 block jackknife 估计 `beta` 与 `se`，再计算

$$
z_i=\frac{\hat\beta_i}{\operatorname{se}(\hat\beta_i)},\qquad
P_i=P(Z\ge z_i).
$$

这是单侧检验：更小 $P_i$ 表示该 spot 的 SNP-GSS 注释对性状有更强正向富集。它不是该细胞患病概率，也不能跨 GWAS 功效不同的性状直接比较绝对 P 值。论文为此在跨性状区域比较时另外使用 odds ratio。

### 8. 第六步：Cauchy 组合得到区域关联

对已有组织、脑区或细胞类型注释中的 spot，ACAT 将 P 值组合：

$$
T=\sum_i w_i\tan\{(0.5-P_i)\pi\},
$$

再由 Cauchy 尾概率得到 $P_{\mathrm{region}}$。当前代码默认等权，对 $P<10^{-15}$ 使用渐近形式以避免溢出。

实现还在 $-\log_{10}P$ 空间检查 `median + 3 IQR` 离群点；只有移除数大于 0 且小于 `max(1%, 20)` 时才用过滤后集合，否则保留全部 P 值。这是软件层面的稳健化细节，不应误写成论文 ACAT 公式本身。

### 11. 必须保留的解释边界

1. gsMap 检验遗传力富集关联，不证明 spot、细胞或区域对疾病有因果作用。
2. 相邻 spot 表达相关，非致病细胞可能因相关表达而一起显著；论文也明确列为限制。
3. 动物 ST 与人 GWAS 的同源映射会损失信息，尤其不能自动消除物种差异。
4. spot 分辨率由平台和分割决定，Visium 结果不可直接称为单细胞结果。
5. GWAS 样本量和多基因性影响 P 值；跨性状比较应控制统计功效。
6. SNP-to-gene 规则、LD reference、baseline annotations 和 ancestry 必须匹配分析目标。
8. 论文公式/默认值与当前软件快照的差异应保留为 `Partial`，不能为了叙述流畅而隐藏。

### 12. 一句话理解

gsMap 先用空间图与表达学习同质微域，以 rank 聚合得到每个位置的基因特异性，再把这些空间变化的基因权重映射到 SNP 和 LD score，最后用条件 S-LDSC 判断哪个 spot 富集某性状的遗传信号，并用 Cauchy 组合汇总区域证据。

## gsMap: Genetically Informed Spatial Mapping of Cells for Complex Traits - Paper Summary

### Publication Information
- **Title**: Spatially resolved mapping of cells associated with human complex traits
- **Authors**: Liyang Song, Wenhao Chen, Junren Hou, Minmin Guo & Jian Yang
- **Journal**: Nature (2025)
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08757-x
- **GitHub**: https://github.com/JianYang-Lab/gsMap

### 1. Motivation and Novelty

#### 1.1 Biological Problem Being Addressed

The composition and spatial organization of cells within tissues are critical to their function and can serve as health status indicators. While single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) has enabled identification of trait-associated cells, it lacks spatial positional information. There is a critical gap in methods that can identify disease-relevant cells and map their spatial distribution within tissues at single-cell resolution.

> "Depicting spatial distributions of disease-relevant cells is crucial for understanding disease pathology" (Abstract)

> "The composition and spatial organization of cells within a tissue are critical to its function and can also serve as indicators of its health status" (Page 1)

#### 1.2 Limitations of Existing Approaches

**Current scRNA-seq based methods:**
- Can identify trait-associated cells through GWAS integration (e.g., scDRS, MAGMA-based approaches)
- Cannot map spatial distribution due to absence of cell spatial coordinates
- When applied to spatial transcriptomics (ST) data, have limited power due to:
  - Lack of modeling for cell spatial coordinates
  - High technical noise in ST data
  - No consideration of spatial context

> "Although these methods can pinpoint trait-associated cells, they encounter challenges in mapping the spatial distribution of these identified cells owing to the lack of cell spatial positional information in scRNA-seq data." (Page 1)

**Previous spatial domain analyses:**
- Operate at domain level, not single-cell resolution
- Cannot profile fine-scale spatial distribution of trait-associated cells
- Limited ability to capture within-cell-type heterogeneity

> "Although previous studies have associated spatial domains with complex traits, these analyses are not at single-cell resolution, limiting their ability to profile the spatial distribution of trait-associated cells." (Page 1)

#### 1.3 Unique Contributions and Innovations

**gsMap introduces several key innovations:**

1. **Spatially-aware integration**: First method to integrate high-resolution ST data with GWAS summary statistics for spatially resolved mapping at cellular resolution

2. **Graph Neural Network (GNN) approach**: Uses GNN to identify homogeneous spots by integrating:
   - Gene expression profiles
   - Spatial coordinates
   - Cell type annotations (when available)

3. **Gene Specificity Scores (GSS)**: Novel scoring system that:
   - Aggregates information from homogeneous spots to address sparsity
   - Uses rank-based metrics for robustness
   - Maps scores to SNPs via distance and epigenomic links

4. **Statistical framework**:
   - Leverages S-LDSC for heritability enrichment
   - **Cauchy combination test** for aggregating spot-level p-values into spatial region significance

> "gsMap uses a graph neural network (GNN) to identify homogeneous spots for each focal spot in ST data in terms of both gene expression patterns and spatial positions." (Page 1)

#### 1.4 Significance for Bioinformatics Community

- **Reveals spatial heterogeneity**: Demonstrates that cells of the same type show trait-relevant heterogeneity based on spatial location
- **Clinical relevance**: Identifies brain regions enriched for psychiatric drug targets
- **Methodological advance**: Provides framework for spatially resolved genetic association studies
- **Resource generation**: Creates comprehensive trait-brain maps for 30 complex traits

### 2. Method Overview

#### 2.1 Algorithmic Framework

The gsMap pipeline consists of three main steps:

**Step 1: Homogeneous Spot Identification**
- Graph attention autoencoder learns embeddings integrating spatial and expression data
- Identifies k-nearest homogeneous spots for each focal spot based on cosine similarity
- Forms "microdomains" of similar cells

> "gsMap uses a GNN to identify homogeneous spots for each focal spot in terms of both gene expression patterns and spatial positions. The gene specificity scores (GSSs) of each spot are computed by aggregating information from these homogeneous spots" (Page 2)

**Step 2: Gene Specificity Scoring and SNP Mapping**
- Computes GSS for each spot using rank-based metrics:
$$F_{ig}=\frac{\operatorname{GM}_{k\in D_i}(R_{kg})}{\operatorname{GM}_{j=1}^{n}(R_{jg})}$$
- Maps GSS to SNPs within 50kb of genes
- Incorporates epigenomic SNP-to-gene links (Roadmap, Activity-by-Contact model)
- Final exponential transformation: $S_{ig} = \exp\{F_{ig}^2\} - 1$

**Step 3: Statistical Association Testing**
- S-LDSC assesses heritability enrichment for high-GSS SNPs
- Cauchy combination test aggregates spot p-values for spatial regions

#### 2.2 Key Technical Components

**Graph Neural Network Architecture:**
- Graph Attention Network (GAT) autoencoder
- Paper: 32-dimensional latent space; current-code default: `gat_hidden2=30` (Partial match)
- Dual loss: reconstruction + cell type prediction
- Prevents over-smoothing via attention weights

> "The loss function of the graph attention auto-encoder in gsMap includes two parts: (1) the mean squared error used for reconstructing gene expression matrix; and (2) the cross-entropy loss used for predicting cell types of spots" (Methods)

**Loss Function:**
$$L = \gamma \left( \sum_{i=1}^{n} \sum_{g=1}^{h} (x_{ig} - \hat{x}_{ig})^2 \right) + (1 - \gamma) \left( \sum_{i=1}^{n} \sum_{k=1}^{c} -p_{ik}(\log q_{ik}) \right)$$

**Gene Specificity Score Calculation:**
- Rank-based approach for robustness to technical artifacts
- Geometric mean across microdomain
- Expression fraction filtering (ratio must be > 1)

**Statistical Testing Framework:**
- Stratified LD Score Regression (S-LDSC)
- Heritability partitioning model: $E[\chi^2_j] = N \times \sum_C \tau_C \times \ell(j,C) + N \times a + 1$
- Conditional on baseline annotations
- Block jackknife for standard errors (200 blocks default)
- Cauchy combination for region-level inference

#### 2.3 The Cauchy Combination Test

A critical innovation in gsMap is the use of the **Cauchy combination test** for aggregating p-values from individual spots within spatial regions.

**Mathematical Foundation:**
$$T_{Cauchy} = \sum_{i=1}^{\phi} \tan\{(0.5 - P_i)\pi\}$$
$$P_{region} \approx \frac{1}{2} - \frac{\arctan(T_{Cauchy})}{\pi}$$

**Advantages:**
- Handles arbitrary dependency structures between spots
- Provides analytical p-value calculation
- Robust to heterogeneity in effect sizes
- More powerful than traditional meta-analysis methods when signals are sparse

> "To quantify the significance of association of a specific spatial region with a trait, gsMap utilizes the Cauchy combination test to aggregate P values of individual spots within that spatial region" (Page 2)

**Implementation Details** (from cauchy_combination_test.py):
- Handles extreme p-values near 0 or 1
- Implements outlier filtering (median + 3*IQR)
- Uses asymptotic approximation for p < 10^-15
- Optimized for numerical stability

### 4. Evaluation Strategy

#### 4.1 Datasets Used

**Spatial Transcriptomics Data:**
- Mouse embryonic: 54 sections, E9.5-E16.5, 25 organs (Chen et al., Stereo-seq)
- Human embryonic: 62 sections, Carnegie Stage 8 (CS8)
- Mouse brain: Adult coronal section (50,140 cells, 14 brain regions, 13 cell types)
- Mouse brain MERFISH: Coronal + sagittal sections
- Macaque cortex: 162 sections, 143 regions, 3 individuals (single-cell resolution)
- Human DLPFC: 8 sections, 10X Visium platform

**GWAS Summary Statistics:**
- 110 complex traits (average n=385,000)
- 30 brain-related traits for detailed analysis
- Categories: cognitive, mood, behavioral, metabolic

> "We collected GWAS summary statistics from the public domain for a broad range of complex traits and diseases, spanning eight major categories: autoimmune, psychiatric, reproductive, behavioural, metabolic, haematological, anthropometric and cancer." (Methods)

#### 4.2 Evaluation Metrics

**Method Validation:**
- Tissue-trait associations via Cauchy combination test
- False discovery rate control in null simulations (<0.05 under all null scenarios)
- Cross-platform consistency (correlation r>0.8)
- Biological replicate reproducibility (r=0.83-0.92)
- Adjacent section consistency (median r=0.92 for SCZ)

**Biological Validation:**
- Known associations (IQ→brain, MCHC→liver, height→cartilage)
- Cell density correlation with trait association
- Drug target enrichment analysis
- Pathway enrichment for identified genes

#### 4.3 Comparative Results

**Against scDRS (repurposed for ST):**
- gsMap shows superior spatial resolution
- Better handling of technical noise
- Captures spatial context dependencies

> "We compared gsMap and scDRS using both simulated and real GWAS datasets" (Page 3)

**Key Findings:**
- Cortex most associated with brain traits
- Glutamatergic neurons show spatial heterogeneity:
  - Dorsal hippocampus → schizophrenia (calcium signaling)
  - Superficial CA1 → depression (neuroplasticity)
- PFC 14r medial region enriched for psychiatric drug targets (OR=16.0, p=2.2×10^-10)

#### 4.4 Biological Validation Experiments

**Spatially-dependent associations discovered:**
1. SCZ-associated neurons decline along hippocampus D-V axis (r=-0.51, p=3.5×10^-190)
2. Depression-associated neurons in PFC 14r medial side (OR=6.7, p=3.6×10^-169)
3. Male pattern baldness → clustered epithelial cells in face (r=0.78, p=2.2×10^-13)

**Gene expression validation:**
- Calcium signaling genes (HPCA, PPP3CA, ATP2B1) correlate with SCZ association
- Neuroplasticity genes (axonogenesis, synapse organization) in depression-associated regions
- Hair follicle markers (KRT15, KRT5, KRT17) in MPB-associated cells

**Drug target enrichment:**
- Genes in PFC 14r medial side enriched for psychiatric drug targets (OR=16.0)
- 417 psychiatric drug target genes from DrugBank and Drug Repurposing Hub

### 5. Key Innovation Highlight: Cauchy Combination Test

The Cauchy combination test is central to gsMap's ability to identify disease-relevant spatial regions. Unlike traditional meta-analysis approaches, it:

1. **Preserves spatial structure**: Aggregates information while maintaining spatial relationships
2. **Handles dependencies**: Works with correlated p-values from neighboring spots
3. **Provides power**: More sensitive for detecting sparse signals across regions
4. **Enables multi-scale analysis**: Can be applied to any defined spatial region

This statistical framework allows gsMap to move beyond single-cell associations to identify functionally relevant tissue regions, bridging the gap between cellular and tissue-level disease mechanisms.

### 6. Simulation Study Details

Four null scenarios tested with 100,000 UK Biobank individuals:

1. **Random causal variants**: Varying polygenicity (10K-500K SNPs) and heritability (0.1-0.6)
2. **cCRE-enriched variants**: Causal variants in candidate cis-regulatory elements
3. **LD block-enriched variants**: Causal variants clustered in LD blocks
4. **NSVG-enriched variants**: Causal variants in non-spatially variable genes

> "Under all null scenarios, the false discovery rate (FDR) of gsMap was well controlled, as it did not identify any spots showing significant associations at FDR < 0.05" (Page 3)

### 7. Limitations Acknowledged

1. **Cross-species inference**: Relies on mouse/macaque ST data for human trait mapping
2. **Correlation not causation**: Associated cells may not biologically contribute to trait
3. **Anatomical homogeneity**: Joint analysis limited to homogeneous sections
4. **No pathway integration**: Gene regulatory networks not incorporated in GSS
5. **Brain focus**: Other tissues not extensively explored
